Method and caveats

What the model predicts

The realized variance of SPY from the decision time to the 4 PM close (sum of squared 5-minute log returns). That's exactly what an option's price bets on: the market's version is implied variance = IV² × time left. Features use only information available at that moment: today's volatility so far, the overnight gap, yesterday's / last week's / last month's volatility, the usual volatility for this time of day, the previous VIX close and the day of week.

How it was tested

Walk-forward: to score year Y, models are trained only on years before Y. Model-vs-market comparisons use the moments where both exist (top of each hour, 2024 onward, when 0DTE option history is available). The live model on the checker page is retrained on all data.

How the backtest works

For each day, at 10:00–15:00 on the hour, every call and put within $5 of SPY is "bought" at its last trade price plus an assumed half-spread (1¢ or 1%, whichever is larger) and held to the close. The history verdict uses only days before the trade.

Caveats
  • Option prices are trades, not bid/ask quotes (not available historically on the free feed), so entry cost is an assumption. The cost table below shows how much it matters.
  • Options history covers 2024 onward only, a short and specific period.
  • Some days are missing from the free archive.
  • Past performance doesn't predict future results. Nothing here is a trading recommendation.
