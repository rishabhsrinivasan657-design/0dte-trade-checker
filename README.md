# 0DTE Trade Checker

**Check a same-day (0DTE) SPY option before you buy it.** Pick a call or put and a strike. The app shows what SPY has to do for the trade to pay off, how likely the market thinks that is, how fast the option melts if nothing happens, and whether SPY's real history agrees with the price.

> Educational tool, not financial advice. Quotes are indicative or delayed.
>

## What it shows

| # | Output | How it's computed |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Breakeven at 4 PM** | strike + premium (call) / strike − premium (put). Premium defaults to the **ask**, what a buyer really pays |
| 2 | **Market's expected move** | from the at-the-money straddle: its implied vol re-centred on spot, `√(2/π)·S·σ·√T` |
| 3 | **Chance it finishes in the money / of profit** | Black-Scholes `N(d₂)` with the option's own implied vol (solved from its mid price); profit = same at the breakeven |
| 4 | **Time decay** | theta in $/hour, plus a direct re-price one hour later (or at the close) with SPY unchanged |
| 5 | **History check** | how often SPY actually moved that far: last ~60 days of 5-min bars *from this time of day to the close*, and ~3 years of daily open→close moves scaled by √time |
| 6 | **Verdict** | market odds vs history: history > market + 5 pts → **Cheap**, < market − 5 pts → **Expensive**, else **Fair** |

Plus a chart of **gamma and theta from the open to 5 minutes before the close**, which shows why 0DTE risk explodes at the end of the day.

## v2: ML forecast + backtest (Research page)

**Question:** can a machine-learning model forecast SPY's move from *now* to the 4 PM close better than the options market does, and does that help pick options?

- **ML model** (`research/move_model.py`): predicts realized variance from the decision time to the close (sum of squared 5-minute log returns, the same quantity an option's implied variance prices). Features use only what's known at that moment: today's volatility so far, the overnight gap, 1/5/22-day realized volatility (HAR-RV inputs), usual volatility for this time of day, the previous VIX close, day of week, and time left. Gradient-boosted trees, compared against a time-of-day rule and the classic **HAR-RV** regression.
- **Walk-forward testing:** each year is scored by a model trained only on earlier years (SPY 5-minute data from 2018).
- **Backtest** (`research/backtest.py`): every past day since 2024, at 10:00–15:00 on the hour, every call and put within $5 of SPY is "bought" at its last trade + an assumed half-spread and held to the close. Signals (market odds, history verdict, ML verdict) use only data available at that moment.
- **Decision-first Checker** (`checker/decision.py`, `research/trade_model.py`): an ML **trade score** (gradient boosting on 80k backtest trades, walk-forward by month) rates the exact option as *Avoid* / *Poor odds* / *Typical 0DTE trade*, with the three biggest reasons in plain English, four key numbers, an exit tip, and a **compare-strikes** table (`/api/scan`). Out of sample the *Avoid* group lost ~48¢ per $1 vs ~5¢ for *Typical*. Honest caveat (shown on the Research page): inside a group of trades with the same distance and time left, the score adds little; the ML move forecast adds a smaller real signal.
- **Research feeds back into the checker** (`research/insights.py`, `models/insights.json`):
  - **Trades like this one:** past trades of the same type, at a similar distance from SPY (measured in "typical moves to the close", so 2024 and 2026 prices compare fairly) and time of day, and how they actually did.
  - **Realistic chance of profit:** the market's odds corrected by how often they came true (isotonic regression; it lowered error on later, unseen days).
  - **ML warning:** the model's forecasts lost to the market's, but options it called *Expensive* lost far more (about −18¢ vs −5¢ per $1) than ones it called *Cheap*, so the checker leads with that flag.
- **Position tracker** (`/track.html`): enter the option you bought; every 15 s it shows live P/L, a "hold or sell?" panel (how often holding to 4 PM beat selling right then, for positions like yours, from 588k past moments), and which exit rules worked for similar trades.
- **Exit-rule research** (`research/exits.py`): "Sell by 3:30 PM" roughly halved the average loss vs holding to 4 PM (about −3¢ vs −6¢ per $1), and helped most for far out-of-the-money options. Results also show the number **without the single best day**, because a few explosive days (e.g. 2025-04-09) drive the averages.
- **What-if map** (`checker/scenarios.py`): profit/loss for a grid of SPY prices × clock times (Black-Scholes at today's implied vol), on the checker and the tracker.
- **Results** are on the **Research** page (`/research.html`), generated by `scripts/run_research.py`. All headline sentences there are computed from the results, not written by hand.

## Method notes

- **Black-Scholes, Greeks and an IV solver written from scratch** (`core/`). The IV solver is Newton-Raphson inside a bisection bracket. Newton divides by vega, which goes to ~0 near expiry, so the solver falls back to bisection whenever a Newton step is unusable.
- **Time to expiry:** `T = minutes until 4:00 PM ET / 525,600` (ACT/365). During a 0DTE session every remaining minute is a trading minute. Because IV is solved with the same `T`, prices, probabilities and theta/hour don't depend on the convention; only the displayed IV number does.
- **Spot from put-call parity:** `S = C − P + K·e^(−rT)` near the money. Free feeds can disagree. Yahoo's option quotes lag its stock price by ~15 min, and Alpaca's free IEX "last trade" can be 30 min old. Backing spot out of the option quotes keeps every number consistent with the prices actually used.
- **Quote cleaning:** drop zero bids, crossed quotes, and spreads wider than max($0.10, 25% of mid).
- **Data:** Alpaca's free *indicative* options feed (via `alpaca-py`), falling back to yfinance; history from yfinance; risk-free rate from the 13-week T-bill (`^IRX`).

## Limitations (honest list)

- Indicative / delayed quotes, not the official OPRA feed.
- SPY options are American-style and physically settled; Black-Scholes is European. The difference is negligible at 0DTE, **except in the final minutes**: implied vol jumps (18% → 75%+ was observed), likely because exercise decisions can still react to after-hours moves until ~5:30 PM. The app warns in the last 15 minutes.
- One constant volatility per option; the real market has a smile/skew.
- "Chance of profit" reuses the option's IV at the breakeven (ignores the smile).
- The probabilities are risk-neutral (what prices imply), not real-world forecasts.
- History can't see today's news (Fed, CPI). The daily version assumes moves spread evenly through the day; real intraday volatility is U-shaped, which is why the intraday version is preferred.
- Options usually price above realized moves (the volatility risk premium), so **"Expensive" is common, and that doesn't make selling them free money.**

## Run it locally

```bash
python -m venv .venv
.venv/bin/pip install -r requirements.txt
cp .env.example .env          # add your Alpaca PAPER keys (free account, no deposit)
.venv/bin/uvicorn app:app --reload
```
Open http://localhost:8000. Without Alpaca keys it falls back to yfinance automatically.

Rebuild the v2 data and results (one-time download of ~8 years of SPY 5-minute bars and ~2.5 years of 0DTE option bars; resumable):
```bash
.venv/bin/python scripts/collect_history.py
.venv/bin/python scripts/run_research.py --rebuild
```

Command-line versions:
```bash
.venv/bin/python scripts/show_market.py          # chain, expected move, IVs
.venv/bin/python scripts/check_trade.py call 772  # full trade check
```

## Tests

```bash
.venv/bin/pytest -q
```
213 tests, no network needed (plus `scripts/live_sweep.py`, which checks every live strike against rules the numbers must always obey). They cover Black-Scholes against Hull's textbook values, every Greek against finite differences, IV round-trips including near-zero vega, chain cleaning and parity spot, the trade checker on a synthetic market, the history check on hand-made price paths, and the API.

## Deploy (Render, free)

The repo includes a `render.yaml` blueprint: one web service running `uvicorn app:app`, which serves both the API and the frontend.

1. Push the repo to GitHub.
2. On render.com: **New → Blueprint** → pick the repo.
3. Enter `ALPACA_API_KEY` and `ALPACA_SECRET_KEY` when asked. They live in Render's environment settings and are never committed.

## Project structure

```
core/          pure math, no data code
  black_scholes.py   price, d1/d2, probability ITM
  greeks.py          delta, gamma, vega, theta
  implied_vol.py     Newton + bisection IV solver
  expiry.py          time-to-expiry convention
data/          fetching and cleaning
  alpaca_client.py   live chain (indicative feed)
  yf_fallback.py     yfinance fallback, same shape
  chain.py           load + clean + parity spot + expected move
  history.py         daily and 5-minute SPY history
  archive.py         historical archive for v2 (SPY 5-min since 2018, 0DTE option bars since 2024)
  rates.py           risk-free rate
checker/       combines everything
  trade_check.py     outputs 1–4
  history_check.py   outputs 5–6
  scenarios.py       what-if grid (SPY price × time)
research/      v2
  move_model.py      features, HAR / gradient-boosting models, walk-forward
  backtest.py        buy-and-hold-to-close simulation, summaries, calibration
  exits.py           hold-vs-sell states and exit-rule simulation
  insights.py        similar trades + realistic-chance curves for the live app
  live_forecast.py   live features + forecast for the web app
models/        trained model (move_model.joblib)
app.py         FastAPI: /api/market, /api/check, serves web/
web/           hand-built frontend: checker (index.html), tracker (track.html), research (research.html)
tests/
```
